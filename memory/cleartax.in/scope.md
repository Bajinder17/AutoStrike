# Scope for cleartax.in

**Vulnerability Disclosure Policy (VDP):** Self-hosted
**Primary Contact:** security-reports@cleartax.in

## In-Scope Assets
- `cleartax.in`
- `*.cleartax.in`
- `*.clear.in`
- ClearTax Mobile Apps (Android/iOS)
- Black by ClearTax Mobile Apps

## Out-of-Scope / Prohibited
- No DDoS or disruptive testing.
- No automated scanners that impact performance.
- Do not access, modify, or delete data belonging to other users.
- No public disclosure without permission.

## Reward Policy
- Discretionary rewards for high severity issues.

## Focus Areas
- IDOR (Sensitive financial data: PAN, Aadhaar).
- Broken Authentication.
- Data Leaks.
- SSRF in financial reporting/import features.
